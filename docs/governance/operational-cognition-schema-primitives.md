# Operational Cognition Schema Primitives

## Purpose

Define the minimum native cognition primitives required for OFFICINA operational cognition implementation.

The objective is not ontology completeness.

The objective is operational cognition stability, reconstructability, synchronization, and implementation viability.

---

# Design Principle

Operational cognition primitives must remain:

- operationally meaningful;
- implementation-oriented;
- reconstructable;
- synchronization-compatible;
- minimally sufficient;
- structurally extensible.

OFFICINA prioritizes operational utility over taxonomic complexity.

---

# Primitive Model

The cognition substrate operates through:

- nodes;
- edges;
- lineage;
- operational coordinates;
- stabilization state;
- persistence semantics.

---

# Node

A node represents a stabilized operational cognition unit.

Nodes may represent:

- architectural decisions;
- implementation milestones;
- governance artifacts;
- operational workflows;
- synchronization models;
- systems;
- schemas;
- runtime concepts;
- cognition structures.

A node must possess:

- operational significance;
- continuity value;
- reconstructable meaning;
- persistence justification.

---

# Edge

An edge represents an operational relationship between nodes.

Edges define:

- dependency;
- implementation flow;
- stabilization lineage;
- governance relationship;
- synchronization relationship;
- operational causality;
- structural association.

Edges must remain operationally meaningful.

Relationship verbosity must remain minimal.

---

# Stabilization State

Operational cognition evolves through stabilization states.

Initial stabilization model:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| exploratory | conceptual exploration |
| emerging | partially stabilized operational meaning |
| stabilized | operationally validated cognition |
| canonical | governance-level stabilized cognition |

---

# Operational Coordinates

Operational coordinates define the current operational position of cognition.

Coordinates progressively refine contextual resolution from broad operational orientation toward narrow operational precision.

Operational coordinates act as:

- cognitive prefilter;
- retrieval anchor;
- context densification mechanism;
- operational attention calibration layer.

---

# Semantic Expansion

Semantic retrieval expands cognition outward from the current operational position without losing operational grounding.

Expansion occurs around operational coordinates rather than isolated semantic similarity.

OFFICINA prioritizes operationally anchored semantic expansion over unrestricted retrieval breadth.

---

# Lineage

Lineage represents operational cognition chronology.

Lineage enables:

- reconstructability;
- implementation continuity;
- governance traceability;
- stabilization history;
- operational evolution tracking.

Repository chronology acts as transitional lineage substrate during bootstrap stage.

---

# Persistence Principle

Persistence is selective.

Only stabilized operational cognition deserves native persistence.

Operational cognition storage prioritizes:

- signal density;
- reconstructability;
- governance value;
- implementation continuity;
- operational significance.

---

# Transitional Bootstrap Semantics

During bootstrap stage:

- GitHub acts as canonical synchronization substrate;
- operational-ledger.ndjson acts as transitional cognition persistence layer;
- repository chronology acts as lineage layer;
- commits act as operational continuity checkpoints;
- conversational interaction acts as cognition refinement layer.

---

# Native Cognition Objective

The long-term objective is transition from:

transitional repository-mediated cognition continuity

toward:

native operational cognition runtime infrastructure.

---

# Governance Principle

OFFICINA does not seek universal knowledge representation.

OFFICINA seeks operational cognition continuity capable of:

- synchronization;
- reconstruction;
- implementation continuity;
- operational awareness;
- governance stability;
- cognition persistence.

---

# MDI+ Principle

MDI+ acts as operational cognition densification protocol.

Its purpose is not verbosity reduction alone.

Its purpose is:

- signal densification;
- operational compression;
- implementation clarity;
- cognition extraction;
- persistence optimization;
- semantic stabilization.

MDI+ may operate before:

- persistence;
- embedding;
- retrieval registration;
- node creation;
- lineage registration.

---

# KISS Principle

Structural simplicity is mandatory.

Every cognition primitive must justify its operational existence.

OFFICINA rejects unnecessary abstraction layers unless they produce measurable operational value.

---

# Canonical Principle

Operational cognition exists to support:

- operational continuity;
- implementation execution;
- synchronization;
- reconstructability;
- governance;
- operational awareness.

The cognition substrate exists to serve operations.

Not ontology for its own sake.
