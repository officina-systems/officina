# Operational Cognition Registration Governance

## Purpose

Define the operational criteria governing cognition persistence inside OFFICINA transitional operational substrate.

The objective is to prevent:

- cognition entropy;
- lineage pollution;
- premature stabilization;
- operational noise accumulation;
- persistence drift.

---

# Core Principle

Only stabilized operational cognition deserves persistence.

Exploration alone does not justify registration.

Persistence occurs only after:

- operational validation;
- implementation significance;
- governance relevance;
- continuity value;
- architectural stabilization.

---

# Persistence Criteria

Register cognition only when at least one condition exists:

- architectural stabilization;
- implementation milestone;
- governance transition;
- operational workflow definition;
- synchronization model establishment;
- persistence boundary clarification;
- cognition lineage significance;
- operational methodology stabilization.

---

# Do Not Register

Do not persist:

- brainstorming;
- weak hypotheses;
- transient exploration;
- conversational filler;
- unstable iterations;
- unresolved speculation;
- low-signal operational noise.

---

# Transitional Operational Ledger Semantics

During bootstrap stage:

- operational-ledger.ndjson acts as transitional cognition persistence substrate;
- repository chronology acts as operational lineage layer;
- GitHub acts as canonical synchronization substrate;
- commits act as operational continuity checkpoints.

---

# Registration Philosophy

Persistence is expensive.

Operational cognition must remain:

- reconstructable;
- high-signal;
- governance-relevant;
- operationally meaningful;
- implementation-oriented.

---

# Governance Principle

OFFICINA prioritizes:

high-density operational cognition persistence
over conversational accumulation.
